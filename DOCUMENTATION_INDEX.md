## 🔑 Key Information at a Glance

### **The 3 Ways People Convert**

1. **Lead Popup** (9-second trigger)
   - Conversion rate: ~16% of visitors
   - Quality: Medium
   - Bonus: PDF with "Web System Blueprint"

2. **Contact Form** (/contact page)
   - Conversion rate: High commitment users
   - Quality: High
   - Best for: Serious prospects

3. **Strategy Call** (Cal.com)
   - Conversion rate: Very high quality
   - Duration: 30 minutes
   - Link: https://cal.com/adszoo/30min

### **The 3 Types of Pages**

1. **Conversion Pages** (3 types)
   - Home page with popup
   - Contact form page
   - Cal.com booking

2. **Service Pages** (14 pages)
   - 8 main services
   - 6 location variants

3. **Content Pages** (23+ pages)
   - 5 industry pages
   - 4 location pages
   - 4 resource pages
   - Blog pages
   - Case studies

### **The Tech Stack**

- **Frontend:** Next.js 15.5, React 18.3, Tailwind CSS
- **Backend:** Firebase, Nodemailer, Formspree
- **Services:** Cal.com, Google Analytics, Vercel
- **Hosting:** Vercel
